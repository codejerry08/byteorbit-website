Who we are

About us

ByteOrbit was founded in Chemnitz by nine students of web engineering. We believe good software comes from a team that communicates openly, shares the work fairly and cares about the people who use it.

Vision and mission

Vision

A web where good software is the standard, not the exception.

Mission

We build web applications around real user needs, with clear design, careful engineering and open communication.

Values

What we stand for

These principles guide how we work together and what we deliver.

1

Users first

Every decision starts with the person who will use the software.

2

Simple by design

If it’s complicated, we keep working until it isn’t.

3

Open to all

Accessible for every user, on every device.

4

Built to last

Clean code, tested and reviewed, so it keeps working.

5

Honest

6

Better every week

Culture and methods

How we work

Four habits that keep nine people moving in the same direction.

Meet regularly

Everyone joins the team meetings. Short notes make sure nothing gets lost.

Plan together

Small tasks, shared decisions and one overview of the current status.

Build in iterations

Short cycles, early testing and improvement based on feedback.

Review and reflect

We look back at what worked and adjust how we collaborate.
